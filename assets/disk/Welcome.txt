Welcome to MayOS!

This file lives on a real FAT32 disk (a virtio block device). Anything you
create, edit, rename or delete here is written to disk and is still there
after a reboot.

Things to try:
  * Double-click folders in the Files window to browse; right-click for
    New Folder, Rename, Duplicate, Copy/Cut/Paste and Delete.
  * Double-click a text file to edit it; press Ctrl+S to save.
  * In the Terminal, type `help`, or run programs from /bin such as
    `hello`, `count 5`, `guess` or `sysinfo`.
  * Drag windows by their title bar, resize them from the edges, and use
    the coloured buttons to close, minimise or maximise.
