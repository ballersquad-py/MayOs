Getting started with MayOS
==========================

Desktop
  - The bar at the top shows the focused app and the clock. Click "MayOS"
    for the system menu (About, new windows, restart, shut down).
  - The dock starts apps. Clicking an app that is already open brings
    its window forward; right-click opens a new window. Move it to the
    left or right, change its size or let it hide itself in Settings >
    Personalization > Dock, or with the terminal: dock left, dock
    bottom, dock small, dock autohide, dock show.

Keyboard shortcuts
  Ctrl+Alt+T   new terminal          Alt+F4    close window
  Ctrl+Alt+E   new file explorer     Alt+Tab   switch window

Files
  Pictures, videos and songs show previews. The button left of "New
  Folder" switches between previews and a detailed list.
  Enter opens, Backspace goes up, F2 renames, Delete deletes,
  Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste, Ctrl+D duplicates,
  Ctrl+N creates a file and Ctrl+Shift+N a folder.

Text editor
  Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+S saves under a new name, Ctrl+A/C/X/V select
  all, copy, cut and paste. Shift+arrows select text.

Pictures and videos
  Double-click a picture (PNG, JPEG or BMP) to view it: the mouse wheel
  or +/- zooms, drag to pan, Left/Right shows the previous/next picture.
  "Set as Wallpaper" (also on the right-click menu in Files, and under
  Settings > Personalization > Your pictures) puts it on the desktop.

Videos and music
  Double-click a video or song to play it. MayOS plays MP4, MOV, M4V,
  MKV and AVI videos with H.264 picture and AAC, MP3 or PCM sound, and
  MP3, M4A/AAC and WAV music.
    Space or K    play/pause           F or double-click   fullscreen
    Left/Right    back/forward 5 s     J / L               back/forward 10 s
    Up/Down       volume               M                   mute
    N / P         next/previous file   0-9                 jump to 0%-90%
    Esc           leave fullscreen
  Videos in other formats (HEVC, VP9, AV1) can be converted on your PC:
    ffmpeg -i input.mkv -c:v libx264 -crf 20 -c:a aac output.mp4

Copying files between your PC and MayOS (easiest)
  MayOS has a built-in file-sharing web page.
  1. VirtualBox: Settings > Network > Adapter 1 (NAT, Intel PRO/1000 MT
     Desktop) > Advanced > Port Forwarding > add a rule:
     Host Port 8080, Guest Port 80 (leave the IP fields empty).
  2. With MayOS running, open http://localhost:8080 in your web browser.
  3. Drag files onto the page to copy them into MayOS, or click a file
     to download it. You can also make folders, rename and delete.
  The terminal command `share` shows the address and `share off` turns
  it off (also in Settings > Network & Internet).

Bringing lots of files at once (a disk made on Windows)
  1. On Windows, open Disk Management (Win+X > Disk Management).
     Action > Create VHD: pick a file, e.g. C:\MayOS\files.vhd, size
     1 GB or more, "Fixed size", VHD. Right-click the new disk >
     Initialize Disk (MBR), then right-click its space > New Simple
     Volume, file system FAT32. (Windows only offers FAT32 up to 32 GB.)
  2. Copy your pictures, videos and documents onto the new drive letter.
     Folders named "pictures" show up in Settings automatically.
  3. Right-click the disk in Disk Management > Detach VHD.
  4. VirtualBox: Settings > Storage > SATA controller > Add hard disk >
     Add > choose files.vhd. Start MayOS.
  The disk shows up in Files under its label (mounted at /disk1). To
  change it again, shut MayOS down and attach the VHD in Windows again.

Keeping settings and files between restarts
  When MayOS runs from the ISO its own disk lives in memory. Add an empty
  VirtualBox disk (Storage > SATA > Add hard disk > Create, VDI, 1 GB+),
  then Settings > Storage > "Set up for MayOS" (or `setupdisk sata0` in
  the Terminal). After that everything you change is saved on that disk.

Screen size in VirtualBox
  MayOS follows the size of the VirtualBox window: resize or maximise the
  window and the desktop fits itself to it. The mouse moves in and out of
  the window freely. You can still pick a resolution in Settings >
  Display; if the dock is ever out of view, move it (see Desktop above).
