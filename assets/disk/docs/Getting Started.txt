Getting started with MayOS
==========================

Desktop
  - The bar at the top shows the focused app and the clock. Click "MayOS"
    for the system menu (About, new windows, restart, shut down).
  - The dock at the bottom starts apps. Clicking an app that is already
    open brings its window forward; right-click opens a new window.

Keyboard shortcuts
  Ctrl+Alt+T   new terminal          Alt+F4    close window
  Ctrl+Alt+E   new file explorer     Alt+Tab   switch window

Files
  Enter opens, Backspace goes up, F2 renames, Delete deletes,
  Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste, Ctrl+D duplicates,
  Ctrl+N creates a file and Ctrl+Shift+N a folder.

Text editor
  Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+S saves under a new name, Ctrl+A/C/X/V select
  all, copy, cut and paste. Shift+arrows select text.

Pictures and videos
  Double-click a picture (PNG, JPEG or BMP) to view it: the mouse wheel
  or +/- zooms, drag to pan, Left/Right shows the previous/next picture.
  "Set as Wallpaper" (also on the right-click menu in Files, and under
  Settings > Personalization > Your pictures) puts it on the desktop.

  Double-click a video to play it. Space pauses, Left/Right skip 5
  seconds, click the bar to seek. MayOS plays Motion-JPEG AVI files with
  PCM sound. Convert anything else on your PC with ffmpeg:

    ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf scale=854:-2 -c:v mjpeg -q:v 4
           -c:a pcm_s16le -ar 44100 output.avi

Bringing your own files (VirtualBox on Windows)
  1. On Windows, open Disk Management (Win+X > Disk Management).
     Action > Create VHD: pick a file, e.g. C:\MayOS\files.vhd, size
     1 GB or more, "Fixed size", VHD. Right-click the new disk >
     Initialize Disk (MBR), then right-click its space > New Simple
     Volume, file system FAT32. (Windows only offers FAT32 up to 32 GB.)
  2. Copy your pictures, videos and documents onto the new drive letter.
     Folders named "pictures" show up in Settings automatically.
  3. Right-click the disk in Disk Management > Detach VHD.
  4. VirtualBox: Settings > Storage > SATA controller > Add hard disk >
     Add > choose files.vhd. Start MayOS.
  The disk shows up in Files under its label (mounted at /disk1). To
  change it again, shut MayOS down and attach the VHD in Windows again.

Keeping settings and files between restarts
  When MayOS runs from the ISO its own disk lives in memory. Add an empty
  VirtualBox disk (Storage > SATA > Add hard disk > Create, VDI, 1 GB+),
  then Settings > Storage > "Set up for MayOS" (or `setupdisk sata0` in
  the Terminal). After that everything you change is saved on that disk.

Changing resolution in VirtualBox
  If the VirtualBox window becomes bigger than your screen, the dock at
  the bottom is cut off. Pick a resolution smaller than your monitor, or
  press Host+C (right Ctrl+C) for Scaled Mode so the whole desktop fits.
